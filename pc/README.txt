Foyer for PC
============

Foyer, the calm TV home screen, on a Windows PC plugged into your TV.

Install or update
  Double-click "Install Foyer.cmd". Foyer opens full screen and starts by itself
  whenever you sign in to Windows. Updating is the same: run the new one. Your
  settings are kept (they live in %APPDATA%\Foyer).

On the remote (an air-mouse remote, or a keyboard)
  Arrows and OK      move and open
  Back or Esc        go back
  Home               back to Foyer from anywhere (closes the streaming window)
  Menu               Settings

Your phone
  Link the PC in Foyer: Settings > Home screen > Foyer Drop. The Remote tab on
  your phone then drives the PC, and Open on TV opens apps on it.

Streaming
  Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Crave, YouTube and the rest open as their own
  full-screen windows (Microsoft Edge, with its own sign-ins, separate from your
  normal Edge). Sign in once in each. Plex, Spotify, Kodi and Steam open as
  their own programs when they're installed.
  Netflix in 4K needs the free "HEVC Video Extensions" from the Microsoft Store.

Make Windows feel like a TV box (optional)
  - Settings > Accounts > Sign-in options: sign in automatically (or use a PIN).
  - Settings > System > Power: turn the screen off after a while, never sleep.
  - Settings > System > Notifications: turn on Do not disturb.
