Foyer Drop

Welcome back

Sign in to send things to your TV.

Send a note

It appears on the TV's home screen within a minute.

Draw something

Your drawing shows on the TV's home screen. The newest one wins.

Send photos

They join the TV's screensaver when it is set to "Photo drop".

Open on TV

Tap an app and the TV opens it. Foyer has to be on screen.

Link a TV

On the TV: Foyer Settings → Home screen → Foyer Drop → Link this TV. Then type the code it shows.

They choose "Create an account" on this page and enter it. Each account has its own TVs, notes and photos.
